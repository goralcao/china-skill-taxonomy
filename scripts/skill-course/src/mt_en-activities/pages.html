这一课，装进四张卡

1
play 两种搭配
球类裸用、乐器加 the
▼

play football / basketball 不加 the。

play the piano / guitar 加 the。

2
动词配名词固定
do / watch / read
▼

do homework、watch TV、read books，别按中文硬套。

listen to music 要加 to。

3
go 与 like 后接 -ing
go swimming / like reading
▼

go + V-ing 表去做：go swimming、go fishing。

like 后接动名词：I like reading books.

4
说作息带频率词
often / usually
▼

I often play football after school. 频率词放动词前。
